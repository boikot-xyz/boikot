Is Mars Ethical?

Mars is highly unethical

Mars is an American company that has been identified as one of the world's biggest plastic polluters [1], has engaged in price fixing [2][3], and has been accused of underpaying employees [4] and misleading customers with false health claims [5]. The company has also been linked to deforestation in african national parks [6], exploitation of cocoa farmers [7], investment in Israeli tech companies [8], continued operations in Russia [9] - being labelled an international sponsor of war by Ukraine [10], complicity in human trafficking and forced labour [11], and connections to child slavery [12][13][14].
