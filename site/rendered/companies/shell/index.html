Is Shell Ethical?

Shell is highly unethical

Shell is a British‑Dutch multinational oil company that has misled consumers with false carbon‑neutral claims [1], engaged in greenwashing and profiteering [2], profited from fossil fuel extraction while ignoring climate change [3], traded Russian gas after pledging to stop [4], paid bribes to Nigerian customs officials to import equipment [5], and conducted surveillance and spying on governments and journalists [6]. The company has repeatedly violated environmental and safety regulations [7][8][9], including the Clean Air Act [10] , causing MTBE contamination [11] and unsafe working conditions at a pesticide plant [12], selling nematicide with undisclosed TCP that contaminated water supplies [13], committing widespread oil spills and environmental damage in the Niger Delta [14][15], evading oil cleanup responsibilities and allowing pollution while ignoring corruption in oil spill cleanup [16][17], and injuring employees in unsafe working conditions [18][19].
