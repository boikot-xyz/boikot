Is Marriott Ethical?

Marriott is not ethical

Marriott is a US hotel chain that has bribed customers for good reviews [1], evaded taxes [2], failed to meet animal welfare commitments [3][4], attempted to silence activists about its animal welfare progress [5], and built a hotel that disrupted Serengeti wildlife migrations [6]. The company has replaced striking workers with vulnerable migrants [7], prevented staff from taking breaks [8], stolen gratuities from employees [9], engaged in human trafficking and abuse against hospitality staff [10], refused to share prevalence of sexual harassment against employees [11], and ignored and profited from sex trafficking [12].
