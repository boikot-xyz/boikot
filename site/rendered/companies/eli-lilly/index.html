Is Eli Lilly Ethical?

Eli Lilly is highly unethical

Eli Lilly is a U.S. pharmaceutical company that has inflated insulin prices through collusion [1][2][3], blocked whistleblowers and falsified manufacturing records [4], manipulated documents to cover up quality control issues [5], failed to disclose state lobbying spending [6], committed bribery to secure contracts in foreign markets [7], offered illegal kickbacks to doctors to boost prescriptions [8][9][10], and illegally promoted drugs for unapproved uses [11][12][13][14][15]. The company has also engaged in sex discrimination and harassment of female employees [16].
