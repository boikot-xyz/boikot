Yes, that's right - according to cash-savvy foodies, the popular chain Wetherspoons is now offering a bargain deal, serving a full English for £1.99.

The incredible offer, which is available at Wetherspoons' pubs across England and Wales, has slashed the cost of the small breakfast to £1.99, whilst the traditional breakfast sells for £2.99, Mirror reported.
Usually, these popular menu items would set food lovers back at around £3.29 and £3.59, respectively - however, it's worth noting that Wetherspoons prices vary across its different locations.
A pint of Bud Light and Doom Bar is now on sale at an average price of £1.99, whilst a Spoons classic cocktail goes for £2.99.
Meanwhile, a bottle of Beck's Blue is on offer for £1.49 and Pepsi Max has been slashed to £1.49, whilst refillable Lavazza coffee, tea and hot chocolate are now available for £1.29, Manchester Evening News reported.

Savings to be made on food items include a small breakfast - which comes with fried egg, bacon, sausage, baked beans and toast - for £1.99, while their burgers are reduced to £4.99 with a soft drink; those opting for an alcoholic drink will have to pay £6.46.
Wetherspoon founder and chairman Tim Martin said: "Department stores and shops hold their sales in January, so it is the perfect time to have a sale in the pubs too. 
''The range of drinks and food on sale in the pubs is aimed at suiting a wide variety of tastes. 
''This year we have included our biggest selection of low and non-alcoholic drinks.

However, if you want to treat yourself to a cheap pub breakfast or a cheeky drink on the weekend, best be quick, as the offer is for a limited time only.
According to the chain, the sale, which kicked off on Tuesday, January 2nd, will end on Wednesday, January 17th.
