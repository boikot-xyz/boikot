Putnam Five Guys employees sue managers, chain over sexual harassment claims

Andrea Lannom

            Jun 11, 2015
        

Jun 11, 2015

Two former employees are suing a newly-opened business in Hurricane, alleging a manager at the restaurant sexually harassed them.Putnam County residents Macey Lewis and Brittany Mercer-Gregory, who both used to work at Five Guys in Hurricane, filed two separate lawsuits Wednesday in Kanawha County Circuit Court against Five Guys Operations, Hurricane general manager Curtis Runyon and restaurant manager Andre Devon “Dre” Fleming, of Kanawha County.Filed by their attorneys John Einreinhofer and Gregory Courtright, the lawsuits alleged Fleming subjected the two women to sexual advances and unwanted physical conduct, creating an intimidating or hostile work environment.

The lawsuits alleged the conduct was so severe that both of them had to quit their jobs.In her lawsuit, Lewis alleged Fleming made inappropriate comments to her such as, “bend over and I’ll make you forget about your boyfriend,” and saying, “I want to wrap you up and put my legs around you while naked.” The lawsuit also alleged he told her “not to tell him she was in high school because it turns him off.”

Mercer-Gregory’s lawsuit alleged Fleming had a “pattern” of making inappropriate sexual advances and derogatory sexual remarks to female employees. She alleged Fleming subjected her to inappropriate comments, calling her “Big booty Brit,” and also physical conduct, alleging he grabbed her from behind, pulled her against his crotch and allowed a co-worker to grab her by her crotch. She said Fleming also allowed the coworker, who was not listed as a defendant in the lawsuit, to grab her chest and kiss her, even after she reported conduct to management.Both of the lawsuits alleged Fleming was not seriously reprimanded for “outrageous and illegal conduct.” The lawsuit alleged Runyon failed to take action against the manager.Both lawsuits are seeking various damages including damages for mental anguish, emotional distress, humiliation, aggravation, inconvenience and damage to their reputations.A call to a Five Guys media spokesperson was not returned Thursday, and a response has not yet been filed in court.Contact writer Andrea Lannom at Andrea.Lannom@dailymailwv.com or 304-348-5148.
