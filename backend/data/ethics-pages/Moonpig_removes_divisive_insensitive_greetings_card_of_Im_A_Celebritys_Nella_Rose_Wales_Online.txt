Learn moreView ImageMoonpig has removed its divisive greetings card of Nella Rose, after images of the card received backlash online(Image: ITV/REX/Shutterstock)Moonpig has removed a birthday card depicting I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out of Here! star Nella Rose, which referenced her early disagreement with fellow campmate Fred Sirieix. The greetings card company appeared to ‘mock’ the star over the spat she had with the First Dates star.The YouTuber aired her grievances with Fred, who said she was offended by his comments about him being ‘old enough’ to be her dad. Nella took offence after she reminded the Channel 4 star she had told her about the death of her dad earlier on.Photos of the Moonpig card made its way to social media, which displayed a cartoon Nella pulling a face surrounded by jungle foliage. The card read: “I’m really offended that it is your birthday.”The design divided opinions on X, formerly known as Twitter, calling out Moonpig for their decision to sell the card. Super TV re-shared an image of the card writing: “So Nella’s going to come out of the jungle and see corporations like @MoonpigUK mocking the trauma she’s faced losing both of her parents recently? Cool. #ImACeleb.”Others agreed that the design was ‘insensitive’, with Brattyn Barbie writing, “Ew its the fact people would actually buy that embarrassing,” while Michael X said, “I'm not a fan of hers at all but this is too much. It's a reality show at the end of the day.”Content cannot be displayed without consentGalarian commented: “I feel like Fred is going to be shocked, by all this too. He moved on and squashed it so fast. I don't think he ever held any bad feelings towards Nella before or after... he wasn't even bothered if u ask me. Just the public triggered.”However, not everyone believed the card was offensive. Tom Yorke tweeted: “It’s not a particularly funny card but to be accurate, she was offended by the concepts of ‘age’ and ‘time’. Nothing to do with her parents.”Big Fisherman C wrote: “I’ve never participated in the Nella pile-on but I think people are being a bit precious. Being offended at Moonpig kinda proves the point they are trying to make with the joke. People claiming to call them out and threatening a boycott are providing them with free publicity.”Moonpig has since removed the card from its website completely. Earlier this week, Nella was briefly removed from camp to visit the show's medics. Viewers were informed by hosts Ant McPartlin and Dec Donnelly at the end of Tuesday (November 28) night's show that Nella had been given a last-minute exemption from the next Bushtucker trial, having been whisked away from camp to seek medical attention.The show’s bosses have confirmed whether the 26-year-old YouTuber and social media star would be making a return, or if her time in the jungle was over. In a new statement an ITV spokesman said: "During last night's live broadcast, Nella Rose required medical treatment on site and was deemed unable to take part in the trial. Nella has since re-joined camp."Article continues belowAn ITV spokesperson at the time appeared to confirm Nella was not leaving the show, as they said she would be returning to camp "shortly", according to MirrorOnline.
